02 — Strategy

Co-investments at Inflection
(Seed to Series B)

Start from first principles. At this stage a round is settled by one lead and a scramble for the remaining allocation, so the standard co-investor optimizes for speed of signature rather than judgment — sign fast, take the price, meet the CEO twice. That is a distribution problem dressed up as an investment practice.

01 — Access

Off-cycle, a year early

Customer intros, hires, the next investor — trust built before there's a price to argue about.

02 — Network

Proprietary, not a pipeline

30 emerging managers sharing deals and one-fifth of our carry, so deals surface from people already inside the company.

03 — Conviction

Diligenced like a lead

200+ companies reviewed per check. Five or six a year out of 15,000+ seen — under 0.2%.

Why the checks stay small

$500K–$1M is never the problem

A small check is never the reason a round doesn't close. Leads keep their ownership, founders keep an independent voice — so the same access converts again and again, into rounds a $5M ask would price us out of.

What we're looking for

The criteria

Second-time founders or operators out of the best startups · $500K–$5M revenue growing 4–10x, typically under three years old · outcome pricing, not seats · teams that move fast, matched by no committee and no reserve games.
