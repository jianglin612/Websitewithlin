North Star

A great founder
Look for a distinctive insight, the ability to recruit senior talent, a track record of owning hard outcomes, and a clear 90-day plan. A practical test is whether we would want to work for this person.
Product-market fit and a path to scale
Look for $500K–$10M ARR, a sales playbook that two non-founder reps can run, and a precise ideal customer profile with a top-two buying priority. We prefer results or usage pricing and look for ACV × potential customers × close rate above $1B.
What strengthens the case
A likely tier-one lead, 3–9 months before the next round, meaningful founder capital at risk, $250K+ in revenue per employee, and a team that ships weekly all strengthen the case. These are supporting signals.
Align the network
Build trusted relationships with emerging managers and share deal flow, introductions, and economics.
Review more companies to improve selection
Aim to see 200+ companies for each investment and use the network to surface more than 15,000 startups a year.
Win outside the process
Build conviction before the formal raise. Use flexibility and speed to earn access before competitive bidding starts.
Help founders ship and sell
Bring in operators who can help with product and sales. Be the first call for an introduction, a hire, or a hard question.
